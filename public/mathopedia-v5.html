Account rules
  1. One account per person — do not share login credentials.
  2. You must be at least 18 years old to register.
  3. Reader — browse, search, bookmark, discuss, flag, cite, take quizzes and track learning. Active immediately.
  4. Contributor — requires a scholar application with evidence of a Master's, doctorate or equivalent, verified by an administrator.
  5. Reviewer — requires a scholar application, declared fields of expertise and a conflict-of-interest declaration.
  6. Administrator accounts are never publicly registered.
  7. Registering sends a confirmation email. The address must be confirmed before the account can sign in.
